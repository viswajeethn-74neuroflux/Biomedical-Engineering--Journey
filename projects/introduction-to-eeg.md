Introduction to EEG

1. Project Overview

This project explores electroencephalography (EEG), a technology used to record electrical activity from the brain.

2. Objective

To understand the basic principles of EEG, how it works, its clinical applications, and the role of biomedical engineers in EEG technology.

3. How EEG Works

Research and explain how electrodes detect electrical activity from the scalp and how the EEG system records this activity.

4. Applications

Research how EEG is used in clinical practice and neuroscience research.

5. Role of Biomedical Engineers

Explore the instrumentation, signal acquisition, equipment testing, and maintenance involved in EEG systems.
